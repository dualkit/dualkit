# V2 baseline: how right are check and lint today

Measured on 22 Oct 2026 with `@dualkit/eval`. This is the starting line for v2. Every number below is a count over private data, so no site, product or snippet is named.

## What was measured

Precision: 20 products (10 repos for lint, 10 recorded pages for check), a sample of today's findings, hand labelled real, handled, intended or unclear. Labels are samples of up to 20 per page and 30 per repo, not every finding. Unclear findings are left out of the figure. The coverage table below shows how many labels still match today's findings.

Recall: 65 cases. Eight bug kinds, eight clean pages each, plus one real-world miss. Each case is a clean page with one Arabic bug planted in it. A tool catches a case if it reports a new warning or error on the broken element. Notes do not count. The real-world miss is compared with an empty page, because its stored draft already had warnings of its own.

The clean pages come from the October bench runs, taken one model at a time in turn so the cases spread out. Four models supplied pages: 25 cases come from one, 15 from the second, 13 from the third and 11 from the fourth. The cases cover seven screens: address form (16), order history (12), OTP (12), booking (10), checkout (10), payment summary (3) and support chat (1). For tracking and number isolation, the injector writes exactly what the check rule reads, so those catches are expected. They show the rule works. They do not show it generalises.

## Against the pass bars

The spec asks for at least 90% of findings real, for both tools, and at least 90% of the miss set found.

| measure | bar | check today | lint today |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| findings that are real, warnings and errors | 90% | 76% | 59% |
| findings that are real, all severities | 90% | 63% | 34% |
| miss set found | 90% | 31% | 0% |

Neither tool is close. Check is nearer on precision. Lint is wrong four times in ten on warnings and about two thirds of the time once notes count. Lint catches none of the planted bugs.

Lint has no rules for these bug kinds. For example, it missed a literal letter-spacing on an Arabic heading because no rule looks for it. The eval also ran lint per file on each single-file page, without project context such as Tailwind or the RTL setup. The shipped `lint()` reads a whole project, so its recall on real projects could differ. Some kinds, such as a rendered date order or the width of a number, are out of reach for a source check either way.

Those cases are kept anyway, because the tools still should catch them.

124 of 183 check labels and 218 of 343 lint labels still match a finding in today's run. Precision is computed on the labels that match. The unlabelled column counts today's findings that no label covers.

11 of the 74 real check labels no longer match. That could be a regression in check or drift in the replayed recordings. It is not known yet. Most lost lint labels were handled or intended findings.

## Check against the 21 Oct figures

On 21 Oct lint was at 34% and check at 80%. Today lint reads 34% when every severity counts, the same basis as before. Check reads 76% on warnings and errors.

The 21 Oct check figure left out two layout rules, layout/physical-spacing and layout/physical-inset (see the usefulness sweep, 20 Oct). Both are notes now, so they are already outside today's 76%. The gap of 4 points is unexplained. The rules package was last changed on 21 Oct, the same day as the earlier figures, so I cannot show that the tools are identical.

## What was dropped

No product was skipped: all 20 had a sample file and ran. Two candidate cases were dropped, because they rendered the same as the clean page or failed to render, and the next page took their place. Recordings that no longer match are shown in the coverage table above, not here.

## What this means for v2

- Check precision needs about 14 points. Fixing arabic-leading and clipped-text moves it the most.
- Lint precision needs a rule-by-rule cleanup, and a decision on whether source rules should judge intent they cannot see.
- Recall is the larger gap. Neither tool caught a single case in five of the eight kinds.
