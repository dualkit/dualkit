# V2 step 2: rules, precision fixes and the reviewer

Measured on 22 Oct 2026 with `@dualkit/eval`, on the same frozen data as the [baseline](2026-10-22-v2-baseline.md). Every number is a count over private data, so no site, product or snippet is named.

## What changed

- Five new check rules: date order (`bidi/date-order`), Latin punctuation in Arabic, currency on the wrong side, a back arrow that points forward (`icons/arrow-points-forward`), and an OTP or phone field that drops Arabic digits (a probe that types into the field).
- Two check rules made stricter: `type/arabic-leading` (threshold 1.45, display text at 22px and up exempt) and `layout/clipped-text` (a line clamp showing two or more lines is exempt).
- Two lint rules moved to notes: `source/physical-tailwind`, at 64% on 28 decided findings, and `source/intl-digits`, which was wrong on both of its findings and cannot tell an intended Western digit from a mistake.
- `dualkit check --review`: a vision model reads the screenshot against review rules picked for what the page contains, then checks each finding again on a crop before it is shown. It is off unless asked for, and it never changes the score or the exit code.

## Against the pass bars

| measure | bar | check before | check now | lint before | lint now |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| findings that are real, warnings and errors | 90% | 76% | 82% | 59% | no warnings left to measure |
| findings that are real, all severities | 90% | 63% | 66% | 34% | 34% |
| miss set found | 90% | 31% | 100% | 0% | 0% |

Recall passes the bar on the frozen set: check now finds every one of the 65 cases, including the real-world miss. Precision does not pass. Check gained 6 points and is 8 short.

Two cautions on recall. The new rules were written for the eight bug kinds in the miss set, so 100% shows each rule works on the bug it was built for. It does not show it finds bugs nobody planted. And on the 10 recorded real sites the five new rules found nothing at all, so they raised no false alarms but have no real-world catch on record either.

Lint now has no labelled warning or error left. Every labelled lint finding is a note, so its warning precision cannot be measured on this set. Lint still finds none of the planted bugs. Most of them are only visible once a page renders.

## The reviewer

Measured on 23 Oct 2026 with free Gemini (`gemini-flash-lite-latest`): 126 calls, $0.00.

| measure | result |
| --- | --- |
| miss set found by review alone | 0% (0 of 65) |
| findings on the recorded real sites | 4, not yet labelled |
| findings across all reviewed pages | 42, none left unchecked by the second look |
| pages too tall to review in full | 9, where only the first 4 screens were read |

The reviewer found none of the planted bugs. Check already finds all of them, so review adds nothing there. Its 42 findings are of other kinds: Arabic that reads like a machine translation (15), icons pointing the wrong way (11), amounts that read wrong (10), cut-off or overlapping Arabic (5) and a date (1). None is labelled yet, so its precision is unknown.

`--review` stays experimental and off unless asked for. Rerunning costs nothing, because results are cached:

```
npm run eval -w @dualkit/eval -- all --review --data <private data>
```

## What is left

- Check precision is 8 points short of the bar. The next gains are in the small rules: clipped-text, arabic-font, faux-bold, unisolated-number and mixed-digits. Each needs more labels before a verdict.
- arabic-leading has 10 unclear labels. A person deciding them settles whether it passes.
- Label the reviewer's findings to get its precision. It finds none of the planted bugs, so any value it has is in what check cannot see.
- Lint needs labelled warnings from its remaining rules before its precision means anything.
